Why Dessau Modul

You wouldn’t sign off on a project with unclear scope. Why do it with your home?

Most interior projects run on verbal promises — a rate that changes after work starts, a delivery date nobody owns, a carpenter who disappears after the invoice clears.

Dessau Modul works differently. We agree the scope with you, fix the price before work begins, and one team stays accountable from start to finish.

The idea behind the name

Inspired by the Bauhaus. Made in our own factories.

A century ago, the Bauhaus school in Dessau, Germany, showed that furniture could be designed with precision, made with method, and built in a factory without losing its craft. Our name is a tribute to that way of working. Dessau Modul is an independent Indian business with no connection to the school or the town.

  1. Precision

    Measured, never guessed. Every panel is cut by CNC machines from a dimensioned production drawing.

  2. Method

    The same process for every piece. Nine checkpoints, from design analysis to dispatch, with no steps skipped.

  3. Perfection

    Finished before it reaches your home. Edge banding, PU coating and inspection happen in our factory, not on your site.

The squares and circle in our logo are a nod to the same design language.

Merino Senses · Authorised stockist, Pune

The finish isn’t outsourced either.

Dessau Modul is the authorised Merino Senses stockist for Pune — a national brand’s real-wood-finish panels, sold and fitted by the one team that also builds your cabinets.

Most vendors either sell a branded panel or manufacture the box; we control both ends, so the finish you saw in the sample and the finish that lands in your home are the same batch, from the same supplier relationship.

Few other stockists exist in this line across the city.
